Privacy at MaliPen

Privacy Policy

MaliPen is built as a local-first screen annotation tool. This policy explains what the application accesses, what stays on your device, and when it may connect to third-party services.

Effective date: September 30, 2026
✓ No account required

Use MaliPen without creating a profile or signing in.

↓ Local processing

Screen content, drawings, annotations, and captures stay on your device.

× No ad tracking

No advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or profiling services are included.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the MaliPen desktop application and explains how MaliPen handles information when you use its drawing, annotation, whiteboard, screenshot, and freeze-screen features.

2. Accounts and Personal Information

MaliPen does not require an account. The application does not ask you to provide and does not intentionally collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, or account credentials.

MaliPen does not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Screen Content and Capture Features

MaliPen includes screen annotation, screenshot, region capture, and freeze-screen features. When you use these features, the application accesses the content currently displayed on your screen only to provide the function you requested.

Screen content is processed locally on your device. MaliPen does not upload captured screens, screenshots, or screen content to MaliPen servers.

4. Drawings, Annotations, and Local Files

  • Drawings and annotations: Pen strokes, text, shapes, whiteboard content, and similar material are processed locally and are not automatically transmitted to external servers.
  • Screenshots: A screenshot is copied to your local clipboard or saved to a location you choose, depending on your selected option.
  • Settings: Preferences such as colors, brush sizes, shortcuts, and screenshot settings are stored locally in the application's settings file on your device.

You remain in control of the files you save and the locations where you save them. MaliPen does not automatically upload this content to cloud storage.

5. Analytics, Advertising, and Crash Reporting

MaliPen does not include advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, telemetry services, or remote crash-reporting services. Your screen content, drawings, annotations, and screenshots are not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

6. Internet Access and External Links

MaliPen automatically checks for application updates after launch through GitHub-hosted release infrastructure and may download an update when one is available. The application may also open a website in your default browser when you explicitly select an external link, such as a developer website, GitHub, Instagram, or MaliAcademy link.

These operations do not upload the contents of your screen, drawings, annotations, or screenshots. Third-party providers may receive standard connection information, such as an IP address, according to their own privacy policies.

7. Operating System and Distribution Services

Operating-system and distribution services—including Microsoft Store, GitHub release infrastructure, and platform update or security services—may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies. MaliPen does not control how those independent services handle data.

8. Children's Privacy

MaliPen does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the application does not require accounts or intentionally collect personal data, it does not create age-based personal profiles.

9. Your Security and Control

Because MaliPen processes user-created content locally, you are responsible for protecting sensitive information shown on your screen and for securing files saved on your device or to any location you select.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MaliPen's features change or when legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
